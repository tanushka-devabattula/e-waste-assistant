E-WASTE GUIDANCE

Electronic devices should not be treated like ordinary household waste.
Responsible handling helps recover valuable materials and reduces
environmental harm.

REPAIR
If an electronic device is still usable but has a problem, repair can
extend its useful life and delay replacement. Users should prefer
qualified or authorized repair professionals for repairs involving
electrical components or batteries.

REUSE
A working device can continue to provide value through extended personal
use, secondary use, or other safe reuse options.

DONATE OR REFURBISH
Working or repairable devices may be suitable for donation or
refurbishment. Refurbishment can restore devices so they can be used
again instead of being discarded.

RECYCLE
Devices that are no longer practical to repair or reuse may be sent to
appropriate electronic-waste recycling facilities. Responsible recycling
can help recover useful materials.

RESPONSIBLE DISPOSAL
Electronic devices and batteries should not be placed in regular
household waste. Users should use appropriate e-waste collection or
authorized disposal channels.

BATTERY SAFETY
Swollen, leaking, overheating, or visibly damaged batteries can present
serious safety risks. Do not attempt DIY handling of a damaged battery.
Stop using the device when appropriate and seek professional or
authorized handling.

SUSTAINABILITY
Extending the useful life of electronic products can reduce premature
replacement and the amount of e-waste generated. Reuse, refurbishment,
repair, and responsible recycling support more sustainable consumption
of electronic products.
